How It Works

A clear, four-step staffing process

  1. 01

    Tell Us Your Requirement

    Share the roles, headcount, location and timeline. We map your standards, cuisine style and shift pattern.

  2. 02

    We Source & Screen Candidates

    We shortlist from our hospitality talent pool and verify experience, skills, documents and references.

  3. 03

    Candidate Selection

    You interview or trial the shortlist. We coordinate schedules, trade tests and feedback end to end.

  4. 04

    Staff Deployment

    Selected professionals join on the agreed date, with follow-up support and replacement backup.
