About Us

Hospitality is the only industry we staff

Maan World Hospitality is a hospitality staffing and manpower solutions company. We supply trained, reliable and service-ready professionals to hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés, clubs, banquet companies, caterers and institutional kitchens.

Our Story

Built by people who understand service pressure

Hospitality businesses lose revenue and reputation when a station is unmanned, a banquet is under-staffed or a kitchen runs short before a peak weekend. We exist to close that gap. Our team works exclusively within hospitality, which means we recruit against real operational realities — brigade structures, shift patterns, cuisine specialisation, grooming standards and guest-facing conduct.

  • ✓Hospitality-only recruitment focus
  • ✓Screened, verified candidate pool
  • ✓Permanent, temporary and bulk staffing
  • ✓Nationwide sourcing and deployment

One partner for kitchen, service, rooms and events

From a single specialist chef to a complete pre-opening workforce.

Our Mission

Dependable manpower, delivered without drama

To be the hospitality workforce partner operators call first — supplying trained professionals quickly, screening them properly and standing behind every placement we make.

“Great hospitality is delivered by people. Our job is to make sure the right people are in position, on time, every time.”

What We Stand For

Values that show up on the floor

Professionalism

Documented processes, punctual communication and staff who represent your brand correctly from day one.

Reliability

We commit to timelines we can meet, and we stay accountable after the joining date.

Transparency

Service Mindset

Hospitality is judged in seconds. We select for attitude and grooming as much as skill.
